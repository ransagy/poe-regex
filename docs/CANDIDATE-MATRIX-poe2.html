Legend — what the numbers and labels mean
lenCandidate length in characters, including anchors and wildcard dots.
minForeign modeled lines that are minion, allies, or presence lines.
otherAll other foreign modeled lines matching the candidate.
splitsThe fragment contains whitespace. Quoted, it is one line-local term; unquoted, the game would split it into separate ANDed terms.
currentThe fragment currently shipped for this pattern and scope, with its evaluated same-line counts.
knownAn in-game verified reference form. It is shown even when a shorter dominated variant exists.
chosenThe shortest candidate under the current hierarchy. The quoted form is what the compiler emits; the inner length excludes the two quotes.
coveragestrict, minion, current-only, or none. current-only means no shorter form exists in the searched grammar; the shipped entry stays.
saveQuoted characters saved against the current fragment.
noneNo candidate passes that profile after the whole grammar is evaluated. It does not mean the mod is unresolved.
floorThe shortest raw candidate regardless of collisions. Diagnostic only — never a shipping proposal.
per-classEvery applicable class produces the same result; the classes are listed. No class is singled out.
classClass results differ. Each row lists the classes that share that result.
